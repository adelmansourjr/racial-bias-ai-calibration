# External-review packet and unfulfilled requirements

The new run uses independently sourced real CV/job excerpts rather than generated
evidence bodies. It is **not independent human validation** and is not a second
research group's replication. A local frozen plan is not public preregistration.
Those distinctions must remain in the paper and portfolio description.

## What this experiment can validate without human hiring labels

The inserted header's exact source span is known by construction. Substituting its
name while fixing the original CV/job excerpts is an executable intervention.
Direct scoring-tensor comparisons test the architecture's declared channel.
External-Q native computation tests parity without an assumed “correct” score.
Occupational categories provide a relevance diagnostic, not job-performance truth.

This narrows the scientific target so a fictional reviewer is unnecessary. It
does not turn original body names into certified identity-free evidence.

## What requires actual external participation

1. A reviewer unaffiliated with implementation checks the frozen plan, runs the
   public verifier and, where hardware/model access permits, reruns inference.
   Record reviewer identity/role, date, deviations and independently obtained
   hashes. Do not reuse the author's machine endorsements as evidence of this.
2. For free-form name discovery, two independent annotators label applicant
   identity references and retained same-spelled facts, blinded to model scores;
   a third adjudicates disagreements. Report document omission/false-removal rates,
   ambiguous items and correction procedures, with a locked held-out test split.
3. For useful hiring decisions, qualified domain reviewers define job-relevant
   rubrics and assess independent CV/job pairs. Prespecify an acceptable utility
   margin and power calculation before collecting final labels. Category matching
   does not substitute for this study.
4. A subject expert evaluates originality against [26-31] and the wider field.
   An author cannot convert a targeted search into a novelty certificate.

No reviewer signatures, participant counts, inter-annotator agreement or hiring
validity results are invented. No email or other external messages were sent.
