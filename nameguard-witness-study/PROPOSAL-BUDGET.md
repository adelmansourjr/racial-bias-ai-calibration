# Development finding and frozen follow-up

The originally frozen prompt and five-letter decision selected a verified
record in four of twelve definite development cases. The full-coverage
threshold failed on development. Examining the already logged development
scores showed that the model frequently selected E even when it ranked the
correct actual record highest or second highest. Restricting ranked proposals
to letters with an actual source record gives 7/12 at budget one, 11/12 at
budget two, and 12/12 at budget three. These figures select the policy and
carry no held-out weight.

Before test inference, we add a predeclared primary feasibility check for the
revised policy. Rank only actual source pointers, using the same protection
and original prompt. Check the first two pointers. Let the checker derive
conflict, missingness, and any terminal status from the supplied ledger.
Preserve the original five-letter policy and its outcomes. Evaluate budgets
one to four to show where gains come from. At budget four all available
records have been searched, so its result is equivalent to the deterministic
registry comparator on this benchmark.

The revised policy passes if all 96 held-out conditions have all four names
admitted with identical five logits and report bytes, all 31 aligned arrays
match at every planned direct comparison, zero reports are incorrectly
definitive and at least 75% of the verifiable conditions receive a definitive
report with correct record IDs. Evaluate every held-out condition and report
all exclusions. Evidence sensitivity is measured by successes on paired
status flips, evidence deletion, subject swaps and later contradictory
records. The selection and threshold were specified after development and
before test inference in this workspace. This is a local freeze.
